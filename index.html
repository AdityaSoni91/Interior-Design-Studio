ABOUT US

Designing Spaces. Building Stories.

At Innova Interiors, we believe that every space has a story waiting to be told. Founded in 2015, we started with a simple idea — to create beautiful, functional and timeless interiors that reflect the unique personality of each client.

Today, we are a full-service interior design studio, working with homeowners, businesses and developers to transform spaces into extraordinary experiences.
